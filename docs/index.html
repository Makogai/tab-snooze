Close now,
reopen later

Snooze the tab you are on. It closes immediately and comes back when you choose — no account, no cloud, everything stays on your device.

Get on GitHub View source

Built for focus

Park tabs you are not ready to close forever — read-later, end-of-day cleanup, or remind me tonight.

⏰

Quick presets

15 minutes, 1 hour, 3 hours, this evening, or tomorrow morning — one click from the toolbar.

📋

Snoozed list

See what is waiting, wake a tab early, or cancel. Badge shows how many tabs are snoozed.

🔒

Private and offline

Only URLs and titles are stored locally in Chrome. No analytics and no backend.

How it works

  1. Open the tab you want to park.
  2. Click Tab Snooze and pick a time.
  3. The tab closes; when the alarm fires it reopens with a notification.
