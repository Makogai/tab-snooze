Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-05-18

What this extension does

Tab Snooze saves the URL and title of tabs you snooze, plus when they should reopen. Data is stored in Chrome local storage on your device only.

What we do not collect

  • No account or sign-in
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No cloud server operated by this project
  • No sale of personal data

Permissions

  • tabs — read the active tab to snooze it and reopen it later
  • alarms — wake snoozed tabs at the chosen time
  • storage — persist your snooze list locally
  • notifications — optional alert when a tab reopens

Open source

Source code is available on GitHub. You can review exactly what runs in your browser.
